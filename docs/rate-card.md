# Viewora Pricing & Rate Card 2026

Transcribed from "Viewora Pricing & Rate Card 2026" (Oct 27, 2026). All prices are in KES. They are proposed starting points to test, not verified market rates.

This is the **source document** for `src/quote/seed/rateTable.v1.ts`. When the card changes, update this file and the seed together. Once the admin rate editor exists (Phase 2), live prices are edited there instead.

## How pricing works

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Scene | One 360° point (a room or an area) |
| Homes | KES 450-550 per scene, minimum job KES 4,500 |
| Businesses | KES 800-1,000 per scene, minimum job KES 7,000 |
| Industry not listed | Scenes × the rate above, then sanity-check against what the shoot earns the client |
| Price floor | Your hours × KES 1,500-2,000 plus transport |
| Maximum discount | 20% in total; beyond that give extra scenes or free hosting instead |
| Included in every job | 48-72 hour delivery, shareable link, 1 revision round, 12 months hosting |

## Terms and tax

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| Payment | 50% deposit by M-Pesa, balance on delivery; even corporates pay the deposit |
| Cancellation | Rescheduling or cancellation fee within 48 hours of the shoot |
| VAT | 16% standard rate; registration compulsory at KES 5 million taxable turnover in 12 months |
| Invoicing | eTIMS e-invoicing applies to every business, VAT-registered or not |
| Withholding tax | Corporates often deduct about 5% from professional fees; confirm with an accountant |
| Quotes | State on every quote whether the price includes VAT |
| Currency | Quote diaspora, NGO and embassy clients in USD with a small exchange buffer |
